# Quick Demo Explanation - Score Calculation

## The Formula (Simple Version)

**Total Score = Average of (Raw Score ÷ Max Score) × 100 for each criterion**

---

## Example Walkthrough

### Given Scores:
- Stage Presence: **2.00** out of **4.00**
- Choreography: **1.50** out of **3.00**
- Confidence: **2.00** out of **3.00**

### Step 1: Convert to Percentages
- Stage Presence: (2.00 ÷ 4.00) × 100 = **50%**
- Choreography: (1.50 ÷ 3.00) × 100 = **50%**
- Confidence: (2.00 ÷ 3.00) × 100 = **66.67%**

### Step 2: Average the Percentages
- Total = (50 + 50 + 66.67) ÷ 3 = **55.56%**

---

## Key Points to Emphasize

1. **Each criterion is converted to a percentage** - Makes them comparable
2. **We average the percentages** - Each criterion has equal weight
3. **Result is always 0-100%** - Easy to understand scale
4. **100% = perfect score on all criteria**

---

## One-Sentence Explanation

*"We convert each raw score to a percentage based on its maximum value, then average all the percentages to get the total score."*
